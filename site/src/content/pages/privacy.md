## Browsing this website

This website presents information about Curated Collections and links to research collections. The website itself does not include advertising trackers or analytics scripts. Hosting providers may process request information to deliver and secure the website.

## Enquiries and registration

Registration and enquiry links take you to the relevant external service or open your email application. Review that service’s privacy information before providing personal information.

## External websites

Collection websites and partner services have their own privacy arrangements. Review their policies when following a link from this website.

[Contact the project](../contact/) if you need more information.
