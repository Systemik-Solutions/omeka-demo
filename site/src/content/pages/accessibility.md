## An accessible experience

This website uses semantic page structure, keyboard-accessible navigation, visible focus indicators and responsive layouts. Images include alternative text where they convey content. Motion respects your device’s reduced-motion preference.

The gallery can be filtered using a keyboard. Its results are announced to assistive technology, and all collections remain available when JavaScript is disabled.

## Report a barrier

If you encounter a problem, please include the page address, what you were trying to do, and the browser or assistive technology you were using.

[Contact the project](../contact/)

## External collection websites

The gallery links to independently maintained collection websites. Their accessibility features and support arrangements may differ.
