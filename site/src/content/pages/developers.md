## Build with open infrastructure

The collection platform uses Omeka S. Its resource templates, vocabularies and APIs support structured research data and relationships between records.

[Read the Omeka S developer documentation](https://omeka.org/s/docs/developer/)

## Specialised modules

The existing project catalogue includes modules for extending collection discovery and presentation:

- [Node Graph](https://github.com/Systemik-Solutions/OmekaS-NodeGraph) — interactive visualisations of items and their relationships.
- [Glycerine IIIF Viewer](https://github.com/Systemik-Solutions/OmekaS-GlycerineViewer) — embed IIIF manifests in collection pages.
- [Mapping Extensions](https://github.com/Systemik-Solutions/OmekaS-MappingExtensions) — additional mapping configuration and visualisation modes.

## Working with collection data

Use established vocabularies where they meet the research need, and document custom classes and properties. Review the source collection’s permissions and conditions before reusing its data.

## Contribute or collaborate

For an integration, a module requirement or technical implementation support, include a description of the collection and the functionality you need when making an enquiry.
