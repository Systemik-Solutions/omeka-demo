## Support at each stage

Curated Collections brings together platform implementation, migration, training and technical support. The work is shaped around your collection and the support your team needs.

### Collection planning and implementation

Translate research requirements into a collection structure, metadata model and public website. Support covers the stages of implementation, from an initial plan to reviewing the published experience.

### Migration of existing collections

Review existing data formats and map records to Omeka S. Where appropriate, connect metadata with established linked open data vocabularies used in HASS research.

### Design and discovery

Present collections through accessible websites with search, relationships, maps and other collection discovery tools.

### Training and technical support

Build the skills to maintain collection content through training and documentation, with consulting and development support for specialist requirements.
