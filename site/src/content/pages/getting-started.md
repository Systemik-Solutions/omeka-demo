## Start with your research

Whether you are developing a new collection or moving an existing website, begin with what your research needs to make available. Curated Collections supports researchers through planning, implementation and publishing.

### 1. Tell us about your collection

Outline your research project, the material you want to publish, your intended audience and any existing website or data. Include your timeline and the support you are looking for.

### 2. Plan the structure

Work through your metadata, relationships, access requirements and presentation. This is also the time to identify any cultural permissions, image rights or restrictions that need to carry through to the published collection.

### 3. Build and review

Prepare or import your data into Omeka S, configure the collection website, and review the content, navigation and search experience with your project team.

### 4. Publish and maintain

Use training and documentation to manage your collection. Discuss ongoing support, ownership and sustainability as part of the implementation.

## Already have a collection?

Existing formats can be reviewed for migration into Omeka S. Share a sample of your data and describe how the current collection is organised.
